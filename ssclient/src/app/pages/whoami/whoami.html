Why I made this project and what it means to me.

I'm someone who has spent a lot of time learning things on my own. And over time, I started noticing something that bothered me: learning something from another person feels completely different from learning it alone.

You can watch a hundred videos about something, read tutorials, take courses and search through forums. But sometimes what you really need is someone who has already been through it.

I know something that you want to learn.

You know something that I want to learn. So why shouldn't we just teach each other?

Maybe you know how to play guitar but want to learn a language. Maybe you're good at cooking but want to learn how to draw. Maybe you've spent years working with something that someone else is trying to figure out for the first time.

None of those things need to involve money. Sometimes knowledge is simply something worth sharing.
